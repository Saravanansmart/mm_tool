Frequently Asked Questions About Financial Planning and GST

How to calculate income tax for FY 2025-26 in India?

Our Income Tax Calculator simplifies tax planning for FY 2025-26 by letting you input your annual income, deductions, and exemptions. It’s updated with the latest tax slabs for both New and Old Regimes, helping you decide which saves you more. Include deductions like Section 80C (up to ₹1.5 lakh), 80D for medical insurance, and HRA for rent. The tool provides a detailed breakdown of your tax liability, empowering you to plan efficiently. Always consult a tax professional for personalized advice.

What are the key GST 2.0 changes effective 22 September 2025?

GST 2.0 introduced slab rationalisation, several rate reductions for essentials and clarified transitional treatments around Input Tax Credit (ITC). Businesses should update product/service tax mapping and reconcile ITC balances as per official FAQs. For supply-specific details, review the GST notifications and council FAQs linked in our GST article.

How should businesses treat existing Input Tax Credit (ITC) around the GST rate changes?

Transitional guidance generally allows ITC availed up to the cut-off date to remain, but you must reconcile your ledgers and supplier invoices. For supplies where concessional rates apply only if ITC is not claimed, apply the concession only for future supplies where ITC wasn't availed. Speak with your tax consultant if you have complex cross-period supplies.

Do we need to update MRPs, POS and e-commerce listings after GST 2.0?

Yes. Update POS templates and e-commerce listings with new tax-inclusive prices where rates changed. Clear price communication avoids customer confusion and potential compliance flags from regulators.

What is a SIP and how does it work?

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) lets you invest a fixed amount in mutual funds regularly, harnessing the power of compounding. Our SIP Calculator for FY 2025-26 estimates future returns based on your monthly investment, expected rate of return, and tenure. It’s ideal for Indian investors planning long-term goals like buying a home or retirement. Adjust inputs to explore different scenarios and optimize your wealth creation.

How to plan a fixed deposit investment in India?

Our Fixed Deposit Calculator helps you plan your savings by estimating maturity amounts and interest earned for FY 2025-26. Input your principal, tenure, and compounding frequency to get accurate results based on the latest rates from top Indian banks like SBI and HDFC. Compare different tenures and rates to maximize returns. This tool is perfect for risk-averse investors seeking stable returns.

How to calculate EMI for a loan in India?

Use our Loan EMI Calculator to determine monthly payments for home, car, or personal loans in FY 2025-26. Input loan amount, interest rate, and tenure to get precise EMI and total interest estimates. The tool helps Indian borrowers plan repayments and assess affordability. Explore different loan terms to find the best option for your budget.
