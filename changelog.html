What's New at MoneyMindTool

A running log of calculator fixes, rate refreshes and new tools — so you can see exactly what changed and when.

Tax slabs, interest rates and scheme limits in India change several times a year. Every change below was made after checking the governing rule against the official source, as described in our editorial standards. Spotted something out of date? Tell us and we will review it.

November 2026

  • GST calculator: updated rates and examples to the GST 2.0 structure (5%, 18%, 40%); the old 12% and 28% options are removed.
  • NPS calculator: clarified that the extra ₹50,000 Section 80CCD(1B) deduction is available only under the old tax regime.
  • Consolidated overlapping blog posts into in-depth guides with worked examples (income tax, capital gains, HRA, NPS Vatsalya, SIP, GST, gratuity, home loans, FD vs RD, retirement).
  • Removed review-rating markup that wasn't backed by real user reviews.
  • Capital gains calculator: corrected the long-term holding thresholds and clarified how indexation applies to property and debt funds.
  • EMI calculator: fixed fractional-tenure handling and the interest-share figure. Gold calculator: fixed the Sovereign Gold Bond absolute-return figure.
  • SWP calculator: corrected the worked example (remaining corpus ₹49.18 lakh).
  • New calculators: EMI (all loan types), SWP, CAGR and Gold.
  • Added Share Result buttons, standalone Privacy, Terms and Disclaimer pages, and fixed stale internal links across 19 pages.

September 2026

  • Corrected calculator bugs and refreshed statutory rates across the site; moved remaining FY 2025-26 references to FY 2026-27.
  • PPF, SSY and EPF rates made editable from one place so every page updates together when the government revises them.
  • Explained the SIP return-rate convention on the SIP calculator.
  • UPI charges article updated: MDR confirmed, effective 15 October 2026.
  • Added the About page with our editorial and verification standards, and fixed FAQ answers that didn't match their pages.

August 2026

  • Split the single blog page into individual, indexable guides and linked them from the relevant calculators.
  • Added a Budget Planner calculator and an in-depth article on the UPI transaction charges bill.
  • Accessibility: screen-reader announcements for calculator errors and explicit button types.

July 2026

  • Targeted calculator explanations at the questions people actually search for.
  • Completed header navigation, standardised Indian locale formatting, and added Privacy, Terms, About, Contact and Disclaimer links to every page.
